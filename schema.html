Почему Judge остаётся отдельным: executor tools and worker agents can produce useful signals, but they do not make the final vulnerability decision. The judge consumes normalized evidence, replay metadata, raw artifact references, confidence signals, and budget data, then confirms, rejects, or requests bounded rework. This keeps tool noise and LLM assumptions away from the final report.
